# How to play Borno (simple steps)

## Start the game
1. Double-click **`Open-Borno-in-Unity.bat`** (in `D:\Projects\borno-game`).
2. Wait until Unity is open.
3. In the top menu click **Borno → Play Game**.
   (Or press the ▶ button at the top middle.)
4. The game appears in the **Game** tab. Use the mouse like a finger.

## Stop the game
Click **Borno → Play Game** again, or press the ▶ button again.

## Look like a phone (optional)
In the **Game** tab, click the size menu at its top (it may say
"Free Aspect") and choose a tall size such as **1080x1920**.
If it is not in the list: click **+**, enter Width 1080, Height 1920.

## What to try
| Screen | What to do |
|--------|-----------|
| Home | **Trace** = trace a letter · **Letters** = pick a letter · **Games** = mini-games · small **Parents** button (top right) |
| Tracing | Drag over the grey letter, start at the **green dots**, then press **Done** → 1–3 stars. **Clear** = erase. |
| Letter Match | Look at the picture/word, tap the letter it starts with. |
| Balloon Pop | Tap only the balloons with the letter shown at the top. |
| Parents | Answer the sum (e.g. 7 + 8), see progress, switch sound off/on, reset. |

## If something looks wrong
- Empty grey screen with only "Main Camera"? Use **Borno → Play Game** – it
  opens the right scene (`Assets/Scenes/Main.unity`).
- Red messages in the **Console** tab? Copy them and send them to me.
- Start again from zero stars: **Borno → Delete Saved Progress**.
